JuiceLeft puts the time until your Mac goes flat in the menu bar — in Apple's own battery spot, with Apple's own look — and stretches that time.

This installer puts JuiceLeft into your Applications folder and sets up its helper: the small root job that energy modes and the charging light need. macOS asks for your password (or Touch ID) once, at the Install step. Nothing will ask again — later versions update themselves, signed, from inside the app.

JuiceLeft then opens in your menu bar, taking the place of Apple's battery icon (quit it and Apple's comes back). It runs on Apple Silicon Macs with a battery and macOS 13 or later. If JuiceLeft is already running, it is quit and replaced; your settings and everything it has learned are kept.
